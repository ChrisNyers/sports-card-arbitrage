# V1.1 Build Roadmap & Testing Plan

**Goal:** Implement v1.1 architecture incrementally, test as we build, reach 100+ shadow-mode recommendations.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

### Build Order (Dependencies First)

**Week 1:**

1. **Card Identity Schema** (2-3 hours)
   - Canonical schema with 20+ attributes
   - Identity confidence scoring (0.0-1.0)
   - Validation: `is_valid()` requires confidence > 0.95
   - Test: Can we match same card across eBay/PWCC/Auction sites?

2. **Data Record Wrapper** (1 hour)
   - Every data point needs: source, timestamp, freshness, confidence
   - `is_current()` flag: data_age_minutes < threshold
   - Test: Fetch price → wrap in DataRecord → verify freshness logic

3. **Sold-Comparable Engine** (3-4 hours)
   - Load eBay/PWCC sold listings
   - Validate against card identity (must match on: player, year, product, grade, parallel)
   - Remove duplicates (same card sold twice in one day)
   - Identify outliers (IQR method)
   - Calculate weighted average (recent sales heavier)
   - Return: ComparableSalesAnalysis with median, range, dispersion, confidence
   - Test: Query 10 cards → verify outlier removal → validate weighting logic

**Week 2:**

4. **Acquisition & Sale Cost Calculators** (3 hours)
   - Platform-specific tax, shipping, insurance
   - All-in acquisition cost
   - Net sale proceeds (fees, shipping, insurance, reserves)
   - Test: Buy at $100 → sell at $145 → verify $5.60 net profit calculation

5. **Liquidity Analyzer** (2-3 hours)
   - Historical sales frequency (30/60/90 day)
   - Median days on market
   - Active listings count
   - Probability of sale (0-90 day)
   - Liquidity score (0-100)
   - Test: Cards with 1 sale/month → score <40 → should not recommend

6. **Guardrails Engine** (1-2 hours)
   - 12 data quality + position checks
   - Returns: passed_guardrails (bool), failed_reasons (list)
   - Test: Recommend $500 card → position_size check fails → blocked

**Deliverable after Week 2:** Can calculate complete economics on a single card. No ML, no strategy yet. Just: buy → costs → sell → economics.

**Test:** Pick 5 real cards from eBay. Calculate expected profit on each. Compare to v1.0 (should match or be better).

## Phase 2: Strategy Modules (Weeks 3-4)

### Strategy 1: Same-Card Cross-Market (Priority 1)

**Objective:** Buy identical card at Market A, sell at Market B.

**Implementation (1.5 weeks):**

1. **Matcher:** Query card identity on eBay → match to PWCC inventory
   - Must pass identity confidence > 0.95
   - Must exist on both markets
   - Test: 100 eBay cards → match 30-40% to PWCC

2. **Economics Calculator:**
   - eBay ask price + all-in costs
   - PWCC ask price (expected sale)
   - Gross spread, net profit, ROIC
   - Test: Verify $5-30 profit range on real cards

3. **Filter & Rank:**
   - Guardrails check
   - Min $10 profit, min 5% ROIC
   - Rank by ROIC descending
   - Test: 30-40 candidates → 10-15 pass guardrails

**Shadow Test:** Generate 20 same-card-cross-market recs. Track:
- % that were matched correctly (identity check)
- Expected vs actual profit if we hypothetically bought
- Which guardrails rejected trades

### Strategy 2: Auction-to-Fixed-Price (Priority 2)

**Objective:** Buy card in active auction, sell at fixed-price market.

**Implementation (1 week):**

1. **Auction Predictor:**
   - Current bid + reserve status
   - Estimate final price (based on bid history, time remaining)
   - Test: Predict 30 live auctions → compare to actual final price

2. **Economics Calculator:**
   - Estimated final price (acquisition cost including fees)
   - Sold comps for this card (fixed-price market)
   - Expected profit at different price scenarios
   - Test: Pick 5 auctions → calculate break-even

3. **Timing Filter:**
   - Only recommend auctions 24-48 hours before close
   - Need time to decide and bid
   - Test: Current auctions → filter by close time

**Shadow Test:** Generate 20 auction recs daily. Track:
- Did auction actually sell? At what price?
- Was our final-price estimate within $20?
- Would trade have been profitable?

## Phase 3: Prediction Targets (Week 4)

Instead of one "opportunity score", predict five separate targets:

1. **Probability of minimum net return** (≥5% ROIC)
   - Heuristic: Based on liquidity score and comparable consistency
   - Test: Recommend cards → measure % that hit 5% return

2. **Expected net sale price**
   - Use: Comparable median + uncertainty estimate
   - Test: Predict $145 ± $10 → actual sale $142 → track error

3. **Expected net profit**
   - Use: Sale price estimate - all-in costs
   - Test: Predict $20 profit → actual $18 → track error distribution

4. **Days to sale**
   - Use: Historical median days on market
   - Test: Predict 14 days → actual 12 days → track accuracy

5. **Downside risk**
   - Scenario: Sell at 90% of fair value
   - Test: Calculate max loss on each recommendation

**No ML yet.** Just heuristics + historical data. Test the predictions against real outcomes as we shadow-trade.

## Phase 4: Shadow Mode Operation (Weeks 5-8)

### The Process

```
Daily (automated):
├─ Query new listings on eBay
├─ Match to PWCC / other markets
├─ Calculate economics for each match
├─ Check guardrails
├─ Rank by ROIC
├─ Generate top 10-20 recommendations
├─ Store immutable snapshot (features + predictions)
└─ Report (expected profit, why it qualified, etc.)

Weekly (manual):
├─ Review top 10-20 recommendations
├─ Spot-check predictions
├─ If confidence high enough: execute small trade (optional)
├─ Track actuals (did card sell? at what price?)
└─ Compare actuals to predictions

Monthly:
├─ Calculate precision metrics
├─ Measure forecast error
├─ Identify patterns (which strategies work better?)
└─ Calibrate thresholds
```

### Success Metrics

By end of 4 weeks, we need:

- **100+ recommendations generated** (25 recs/week)
- **Data quality:** <5% identity mismatches
- **Forecast accuracy:**
  - Sale price prediction error: ±$20 (80% of time)
  - Profit prediction error: ±$5 (80% of time)
- **Guardrail effectiveness:** 100% of recommended cards pass all checks
- **Execution readiness:** Operations workflow proven (even without real trades)

### Small Real Trade Test (Week 5-6, Optional)

If recs look solid, execute 2-3 small trades ($100-300):
1. Same-card cross-market (lowest risk)
2. Track actual sale price vs prediction
3. Calculate actual profit vs predicted
4. Verify execution process works

**Do not do this if:**
- Forecast errors > ±$50
- Identity mismatches > 5%
- Guardrails rejecting >50% of candidates

## Known Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Card identity matching fails | Start with very strict matching (must match all 7 fields), relax slowly |
| Comparable data is sparse | Focus first on popular cards (Mahomes, etc.) with lots of sales |
| Costs vary by platform | Hard-code platform-specific costs, test against real purchases |
| Predictions inaccurate | Use simple heuristics first, don't train ML until we have 500+ outcomes |
| Guardrails too strict | Generate 1000 candidates, measure how many pass guardrails. Adjust. |
